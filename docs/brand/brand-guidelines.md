# Sudar Brand Guidelines (v1)

## Brand Foundation
- Name: Sudar
- Tagline: Learns with you, for you.
- Descriptor: The Operating System for Learning.

## Strategic Position
Sudar balances three promises:
- speed for teams,
- evidence-informed design,
- memory-aware personalization.

## Visual Identity Summary
- Logo narrative: "=" access -> "S" flow -> AI flame.
- Palette: Option A (Indigo + Ember).
- Type: Manrope (headlines), Inter (UI/body).
- Theme requirement: equal light and dark support.

## Voice Principles
- Human and clear.
- Outcome-first, tech-second.
- Encouraging and specific.
- Claim-safe and evidence-aware.

## Claims Framework
- Validated product claims: shipped and documented functionality.
- Evidence-informed claims: supported by research literature.
- Future-work claims: explicitly marked as planned.

## Usage Do / Don't
- Do keep copy concise and proof-led.
- Do use tokenized colors and consistent type scale.
- Do represent AI as supportive, not replacement-only.
- Don't use legacy product names in user-facing brand contexts.
- Don't overstate efficacy beyond current evidence.
- Don't fragment color or typography by team or feature.
