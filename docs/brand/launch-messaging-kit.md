## Research Grounded Narrative Inserts

### Digital Learner Twin
Sudar maintains a persistent learner model that combines observed behavior and inferred preferences so guidance can improve over time.

### ALP Direction
Sudar is designed to run as a full platform and to extend existing LMS ecosystems through plugin-style adaptive services.

### Economic Accessibility
Sudar emphasizes low-infrastructure and open architecture pathways so adaptive learning can be realistic beyond high-budget teams.
