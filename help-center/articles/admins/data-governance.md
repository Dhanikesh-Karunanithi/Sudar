# Data governance

**Settings → Data governance** summarises how your org handles learner data:

- GDPR / UK GDPR deployer responsibilities
- FERPA-oriented education record handling
- Retention knobs for learning events, AI interactions, and personalization overlays
- Export format status (SCORM 1.2 shipped; others on roadmap)

Live toggles remain on **Governance** and **Org settings**. Trust pack PDFs live in the repo under `docs/trust/`.
