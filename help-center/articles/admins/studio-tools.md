# Studio tools hub

**Tools** in the Studio sidebar lists utilities beyond core authoring:

- **Gamification** — Org quest and engagement settings.
- **Notifications** — Compliance and learner notification templates.
- **SudarArt** — AI cover generation (currently paused; quality hold).

Each card shows availability and links to help. Integrations and ALP remain under **Integrations** in the sidebar.
