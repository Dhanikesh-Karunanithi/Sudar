# Studio onboarding

When you first sign in to Sudar Studio, a four-step setup flow runs before the dashboard:

1. **Workspace** — Name your organisation (replaces the auto-created personal workspace label).
2. **Team** — Optionally invite colleagues by email with Creator, Manager, Admin, or Learner roles.
3. **First course** — Choose blank, AI-generated, SCORM import, or template; you are routed there on finish.
4. **Governance** — Set content approval and learner consent defaults (editable later under Settings and Governance).

Complete setup marks `onboarding_complete` on your profile. You can revisit org settings anytime from the sidebar.
