# Course export formats

From a course in Studio, open **Export** to download packages for other LMSs.

| Format | Status | Use when |
|--------|--------|----------|
| SCORM 1.2 | **Shipped** | Most corporate LMS and legacy players |
| SCORM 2004 | Planned Q3 2026 | Sequencing and richer tracking |
| IMS Common Cartridge | Planned Q3 2026 | Canvas / Moodle interchange |
| xAPI / CMI5 | Planned Q4 2026 | LRS statement pipelines |

Publishing directly to Sudar Learn does not require export. Export is for hybrid deployments or LMS-of-record elsewhere.

See also: [Data governance](/help/article/admins/data-governance) in Settings.
