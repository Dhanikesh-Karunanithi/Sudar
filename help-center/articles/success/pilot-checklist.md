1. **Pick a crisp outcome**: e.g. compliance completion lift, faster onboarding, or modality adoption experiments.
2. **Seed two champion authors**: they pair with L&D to prove Studio throughput (course time-to-publish, review workflow).
3. **Instrument events early**: confirm ALP event plumbing or native telemetry before scaling learners; dashboards need signal.
4. **Run a four-week feedback loop**: weekly review of Help Center searches, Sudar tutor themes, and assessment scores; feed changes into content.
5. **Document decisions**: policies for AI usage, data retention, and Sudar Agents toggles belong in your internal runbook; link back to Sudar Help Center for user-facing consistency.

For deeper methodology see `docs/PILOT_PLAN.md` in the repository when you need workshop scripts.
