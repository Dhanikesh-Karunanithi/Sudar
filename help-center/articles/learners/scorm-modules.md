Some modules open as **SCORM** lessons in a secure iframe. Your progress and score report back to Sudar once the SCORM runtime signals completion, the same enrolment analytics your administrators expect.

Use Sudar tutoring around SCORM concepts the same way you would textual modules: highlight confusing instructions and Ask Sudar to paraphrase or give an example grounded in permitted content.
