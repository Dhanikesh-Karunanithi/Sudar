**Sudar** is the friendly tutor that rides along while you learn. Open it with **Ask Sudar** (top bar in a course or the floating chat on other screens). Sudar can answer questions about the module you are viewing, suggest examples, and run short study helpers when your organisation allows them.

**Sudar Memory** surfaces what Sudar is allowed to remember, goals, preferences, and learning history fragments your organisation consented to. Adjust what feels right in **Settings** when those controls are offered.

Do **not** paste passwords, payment data, or workplace secrets into chat; Sudar is tuned for learning content, not handling credentials.

When you need step-by-step UI help, open **Sudar Help Center** from the learner dock for modality and navigation guides.
