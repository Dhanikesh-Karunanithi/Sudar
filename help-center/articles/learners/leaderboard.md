# Org leaderboard

The leaderboard ranks learners in your organisation by XP from learning events.

- **This week** — Resets Sunday; sums XP ledger entries for the current week.
- **All time** — Scholar level and total XP from your learner profile.

Open **More → Leaderboard** in Learn or visit `/leaderboard`. Your rank appears even if you are outside the top entries.

XP comes from modules, quests, and engagement milestones—not from opening the app alone.
