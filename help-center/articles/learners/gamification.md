# Gamification in Learn

Sudar ties gamification to **real learning actions**, not empty XP grinds.

- **Daily quests** — Concrete tasks (complete a module, maintain streak, log focused time).
- **Coins** — Earned from quests and milestones; spend in the rewards catalog.
- **Achievements** — Unlock on milestones (first completion, streaks, modality exploration).
- **Leaderboard** — Org-scoped weekly and all-time XP from verified events.

Open **More → Gamification hub** in Learn for an overview, or visit `/gamification` when signed in.
