**SudarNotes** is where Sudar *teaches through conversation*, not only answers questions beside a course. You talk with Sudar on the right; a **living notebook** on the left collects ideas Sudar suggests — you **Accept**, edit, or dismiss each card.

## How to open it

Look for **SudarNotes** in the Learn navigation (or go to `/journey`). Operators enable it with `NEXT_PUBLIC_SUDAR_JOURNEY=1` in production; in local development it is often on by default.

Courses and learning paths stay available. SudarNotes does **not** replace required training — it is another way to learn when you have a goal in your own words.

## How a session feels

1. Tell Sudar what you want to learn (intake).
2. Sudar teaches **one idea at a time** and may suggest a notebook card.
3. Accept cards you want to keep — tools (summary, map, draft) use **accepted** notes only.
4. Every few turns Sudar may ask a soft check (explain in your own words) — not a formal quiz.

## Memory and privacy

Sudar may update **Sudar’s Memory** from these chats (goals, concepts, struggles), under your organisation’s AI settings and your memory cadence preferences. Do not paste passwords, payment data, or workplace secrets into chat.

Claim-level mastery (Teaching OS) can update when soft checks map to domain claims your organisation has curated — see **My Memory** for claim strength when available.
