A **learning path** is an ordered bundle of courses (and sometimes optional branches). Administrators assign paths with due dates compliance teams care about—you will see statuses like **Overdue**, **At risk**, **On track**, and **Completed** when your rollout uses Path compliance dashboards.

Certificates appear when Path or course publishers enable them—check **Progress** for earned credentials and verification links where your organisation exposes them.
