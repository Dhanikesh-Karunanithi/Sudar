Inside a course, open a module. The **top bar** lists modalities as tabs.

- **Read**: Text and structured content. Always available first.
- **Listen**: Audio narration. May take a few seconds the first time it generates; later opens are usually instant if cached.
- **Map**: Mind map of key ideas. You can switch between **Module** and **Course** scope when that control appears.
- **Cards**: Flashcards for quick recall.
- **Watch**: Video when your team published a generated or uploaded video; otherwise it stays unavailable until they add one.
- **Podcast**: Dialogue-style audio when the course author enabled it in Studio.

Use **Ask Sudar** if you are unsure which modality fits your goal, Sudar explains trade-offs without judging your pace.
